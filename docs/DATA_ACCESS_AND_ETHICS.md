# Data Access and Ethics

The 2025 Seoul Survey workbook and its Korean codebook are obtainable from the Seoul Metropolitan Government public-data portal:
https://data.seoul.go.kr/dataList/OA-15564/F/1/datasetView.do

This repository does **not** redistribute the 2,500-row respondent-level workbook or transformed row-level data. A dataset appearing on a public site does not establish that it can be relicensed or freely redistributed under MIT. Review provider terms before sharing.

Local input path: `data/raw/seoul_survey_foreigners_2025.xlsx`.

The results concern recorded self-reports from a survey sample and are not causal, diagnostic, or necessarily population-representative. Sampling weights are not used in the benchmark; evaluate survey-weighted modeling separately when warranted. Preserve privacy when recording screenshots or logs.
